# assets/

Background art for the startup/loading screen.

**Active background: `splash.jpg`** — a photorealistic golden-hour colony scene. To swap in
different art, just replace `assets/splash.jpg` (keep the same name and it's picked up with
no code change). `splash.svg` is a vector fallback you can point `styles.css` at instead.

- Recommended size: **1280×720 or larger**, 16:9, landscape.
- It's darkened by a built-in vignette scrim for legibility, with the title + progress bar
  centred — keep the focal subject slightly off-centre / lower.
- Recommended size: **1920×1080** (16:9), landscape.
- It's darkened by a built-in vignette gradient for legibility, and the title + progress
  bar sit in the centre — keep the focal subject slightly off-centre / lower so the title
  doesn't cover it.

## Suggested image prompt (ultra-realistic)

> A photorealistic, cinematic wide shot of a thriving miniature **hamster colony** built
> into a grassy woodland hillside at golden hour. Real hamsters (Syrian and dwarf) busily
> working: some hauling tiny bundles of grain along little wooden conveyor pathways, one
> running in a large wooden wheel rigged to a rope-and-pulley generator, others peeking
> from cosy burrow doorways dug into the earth. Hand-built rustic structures from twigs,
> bark and pebbles — little storehouses, a watchtower, a water pond fed by a tiny dam.
> Warm low sunlight, soft volumetric god-rays through the trees, dewy grass, shallow depth
> of field with a tilt-shift miniature feel, rich natural color, National Geographic macro
> photography style, extremely detailed fur and textures. No text, no UI. 16:9 composition
> with the main activity in the lower-left third and open sky/foliage upper-right for the
> title to sit over.

Tweak the breed, biome (woodland/prairie/beach/marsh), or time of day to match the vibe
you want. Aim for something that reads as "this little world is real."
