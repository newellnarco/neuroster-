🐹 Neuroster — Game Guide

📖 The story & your goal

  • You are the founder hamster of a brand-new colony. From a single burrow you'll explore, gather, build, automate, populate, and defend a living warren of rodents.
  • There's no "game over" — only growing a thriving colony. Setbacks (a raid, a flood, a lost rodent) are challenges to recover from, not failure.
  • Time only passes while you play (it autosaves; there's no offline progress) — the world is exactly as you left it when you return.

🎮 How to interact

  • Build: open 🏗️ Build, click a card, then click a map tile to place it. Right-click cancels. Buildings take time to construct — awake rodents build them (more builders = faster). Start with a 🕳️ Burrow (housing + breeding), a 🌾 Farm, and a well/pond for water.
  • Care for a rodent: click it on the map (or open 🐹 Rodents), then Feed / Water / Play / Pet — an instant need boost plus bond (affection) that raises its productivity & loyalty. Cooldowns reward periodic check-ins.
  • Click structures to act on them: upgrade a tunnel or Town Hall, repair a flooded mine, clean a dirty burrow, or demolish (50% refund).
  • The alert bar (top) flags whatever needs attention now — click an alert to jump straight to the right panel. The Getting-Started checklist guides your first steps.
  • Controls: Space = pause · 1 / 2 / 3 = speed (1× / 2× / 4×) · 🔊 mute. 1 in-game day = 15 real minutes; hamsters are nocturnal (others differ).

📦 What things are

  • Resources: raw (🪵 wood, 🪨 stone, ⛰️ iron ore, ⚫ coal, 🌱 seeds, 💧 water), refined (🌾 food → 🟡 grain → 🟤 pellets, 🟫 planks, 🔩 iron, ⚙️ steel, 🟦 plastic, 💩 fertilizer), and abstract (⚡ power, 🔬 research). Storage is capped — build Storage so surplus isn't wasted.
  • Needs (each rodent has its own; the bars up top are colony averages): food, water, energy, fun (curiosity↔boredom) and health. Let them slip and productivity, loyalty and breeding suffer.
  • Buildings by category: Housing (burrow, Town Hall), Food, Production (sawmill, smelter, steelworks, refinery, lab, trading hut, conveyors), Wellbeing (playground, infirmary, sand bath, graveyards, caretaker, feeders, toys/maze), Defense (walls, towers, barracks, levees, quake shelters), Mining, and Power (wheel).
  • Rodents & species: hamsters plus recruitable guinea pigs (guards/soldiers), gerbils, mice, rats, beavers (dams) and gophers (fast mine repair). New births can be hybrids blending parents' best traits.
  • Threats: predators (🐺 wolf, 🦅 hawk, 🏴 raiders) and disasters (🌊 flood, 🌋 quake, plus biome-specific tsunami / avalanche / blight / sandstorm / wildfire). Counter them with defensive buildings and guardian species.
  • Neighbours: four animal factions have camps at the map's edges; caravans travel to you for trades and raids.

📈 Progression

  • Core loop: gather → store → refine → build → automate (wheels for power, conveyors, mines, caretakers/feeders) so bigger colonies stay manageable.
  • Levels: rodents earn XP from work → levels → ⭐ skill points you spend on their traits for free. Your highest-level rodent sets your Main Hamster level, which gates advanced content.
  • 🔬 Skills: spend Research on colony-wide upgrades and to unlock new species.
  • 🧬 Evolve: permanent, species-wide upgrades (some need a prerequisite or a higher level).
  • 🏛️ Megaprojects: colony-defining wonders you contribute surplus to over many sessions for huge permanent boosts (production, defense, storage, morale).
  • 🏆 Milestones: concrete goals that pay out a steady drip of rewards.

⚖️ Choices & consequences

  • Kindness vs preservation: unburied dead & untreated injuries crush morale, and so does killing with lethal defenses — but a Vet Clinic lets you heal repelled raiders (morale rises, and they may even join you). Bury the fallen in tiered graves to heal grief.
  • Diplomacy: gift & trade what factions covet to build alliances (allies send aid) — but hoarding their coveted goods breeds envy and invites raids.
  • Leadership: a Town Hall gives a colony-wide boost — but a hall too lavish for everyone else's comforts breeds resentment that erodes morale and output. Keep housing & wellbeing up.
  • Difficulty & resource density (chosen when you found a colony) shape how dangerous and how sparse your world is.

💾 Your hamsters & saves

  • Autosaves to your active hamster — no offline progress.
  • Multiple hamsters: 🆕 New Colony starts a new one; 💾＋ Save As forks a named copy; the start screen's 📂 Load lists all your hamsters — click one to resume its latest autosave.
  • ⬆️ Export / ⬇️ Import to back up a colony or move it between machines.
