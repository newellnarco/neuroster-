🐹 How to Play Neuroster

You're the founder of a hamster colony. Explore, gather, build, automate, and keep your rodents thriving — there's no losing, only growing.

The core loop
  • Gather → Store → Refine → Build. Rodents auto-gather nearby resources and haul them to storage. Build a 🪚 Sawmill/Smelter to refine, then better buildings.
  • Place buildings from the 🏗️ Build tab: click a card, then click a map tile. Right-click cancels. Start with a 🕳️ Burrow (housing + breeding) and a 🌾 Farm.
  • Construction takes time — awake rodents build it; more builders = faster.
Keep them happy
  • Every rodent has its own food, water, energy, fun & health (the bars up top are colony averages). Keep Food and Water stocked; build Playgrounds for fun and Infirmaries for health.
  • Select a rodent (click it, or open the 🐹 Rodents tab) to Feed / Water / Play / Pet it — an instant boost plus bond that lifts loyalty & productivity.
  • Watch the alert bar: it flags whatever needs attention now. Click an alert to jump to the right panel.
Grow & defend
  • 🔬 Skills & 🧬 Evolve spend Research on permanent upgrades and new species. 🤝 Trade with neighbouring factions for alliances (but don't hoard what they covet — it invites raids).
  • 🛡️ Threats shows predators & disasters; counter them with defensive buildings and guardian species. Bury the fallen and heal the sick to protect morale.
  • 🏛️ Mega projects are long-term wonders — pour surplus resources in over time for huge permanent boosts.
Controls & time
  • Space = pause · 1 / 2 / 3 = speed (1×/2×/4×). 1 in-game day = 15 real minutes. Hamsters are nocturnal.
  • No offline progress — the world only advances while you're playing, and autosaves. Use ⬆️ Export / ⬇️ Import to back up or move a colony.
